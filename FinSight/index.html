    About FinSight

    Financial Intelligence Platform

    FinSight is a sophisticated data analytics dashboard designed to transform raw financial data into actionable strategic intelligence. It provides a multi-dimensional view of corporate health, combining historical performance metrics with forward-looking risk assessments and market trends.

    Problems FinSight Addresses

    Risk Mitigation

    Quantifies Credit & Liquidity Risk by analyzing Debt-to-Equity and Current Ratios, allowing stakeholders to identify potential defaults before they occur.

    Anomaly Detection

    Identify unusual Financial Patterns including audit issues, fraud indicators, market shocks, and policy changes.

    Financial Analysis

    Analyze comprehensive Accounting Metrics including assets, liabilities, equity, cash flow, and profitability ratios.

    Dataset Overview

    Financial Forecasting Dataset (2015–2024)

    This dataset contains structured and semi-structured financial data for multiple companies over a 10-year period. It combines corporate financial indicators, stock market metrics, and credit risk scores, reflecting the diverse and complex nature of real-world financial analysis.

    Time Period: 2015–2024 (10 years) | Update Frequency: Quarterly
    View Dataset

    Dashboard Components

    The platform is organized into five specialized modules, each targeting a specific aspect of financial analysis:

    • Dashboard: Executive summary of high-level KPIs and aggregate performance.
    • Financial: Granular analysis of revenue, profit, and earnings per share.
    • Risk Analysis: Deep dive into credit scores and solvency metrics.
    • Stock Insights: Visualization of market cap trends and price volatility.
    • Simulation: Interactive "What-If" modeling to forecast future financial states.
